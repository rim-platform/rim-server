## Sign off your commits

Every commit in a pull request must carry a `Signed-off-by` line with your real name and an
email address you can be reached at:

```
Signed-off-by: Jane Doe <jane@example.com>
```

`git commit -s` adds it for you, using the `user.name` and `user.email` from your git config.
To add it to commits you already made on your branch, run
`git rebase --signoff <base branch>` and force-push the branch.

The sign-off is your statement that you wrote the change, or otherwise have the right to submit
it under the project's license, as set out in the Developer Certificate of Origin below (also
at <https://developercertificate.org/>). There is no separate contributor agreement to sign.

The sign-off must come from the person submitting the change. If a tool or an AI assistant
helped write it, you review it and you sign it off; the tool does not.
